The Iceburg Philosophy

More than coffee. A place to slow down.

Born amidst the verdant tea slopes and mist-kissed hills of Manathavady, Iceburg was conceived not merely as a coffee house, but as an unhurried pause from the everyday.

Here, every cup is measured with intention. Our baristas balance the brightness of locally sourced beans with rich, velvety milks, while our dessert chefs assemble towering faloodas that turn nostalgic Kerala indulgences into modern works of edible art.

“We believe hospitality is the art of giving people time back. In Wayanad's cool evenings, nothing compares to honest coffee, crisp food, and heartfelt laughter.”
